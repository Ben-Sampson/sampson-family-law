About the Project

Why Sampson Family Law exists.

Student class project only. Sampson Family Law is not a real law firm. The creator is a law student and is not licensed to practice law in Oklahoma or any other jurisdiction. Nothing on this site is legal advice. Do not rely on it for a real legal matter.

A class project, not a practice

Sampson Family Law was created for a law school course on AI and the Law. It looks at how firms present themselves online and asks whether AI agents can build a site like that responsibly: with honest disclaimers, cited data, and nothing that could be mistaken for a real attorney.

The design follows the look of many Tulsa-area firm websites (navy and gold, dropdown navigation, practice-area pages, client stories). The difference is that every claim of fact here links to a public source, and every story is labeled fictional.

What you will not find here

  • No bar number, license number, or credentials. The creator is a student and is not licensed.
  • No outcome promises or success rates.
  • No way to send information to anyone. The contact form is a demo.
