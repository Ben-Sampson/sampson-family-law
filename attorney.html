Biography

Steady advocacy, grounded in Tulsa

Claire Sampson grew up in midtown Tulsa, the daughter of a schoolteacher and a small-business owner. In the story of this firm, she watched close family friends spend years and most of their savings in a bitter divorce, and decided early that families deserved a better way through.

After earning an economics degree and a law degree, Claire spent her early career at a mid-sized firm handling contested custody and complex property cases. She learned that thorough preparation was the surest path to fair settlements, and that clients make better decisions when they truly understand their options.

She founded Sampson Family Law to build a practice around those lessons: a small caseload, direct attorney access, written plans and budgets, and a mediation-first philosophy backed by courtroom readiness. Her economics background makes her especially attentive to retirement accounts, business valuations, and the long-term tax consequences of settlements.

Approach

  • Every client receives a written roadmap after the first meeting
  • Agreement where it is safe and realistic; trial preparation where it is not
  • Parenting plans built around the child's actual routine
  • Financial analysis that looks five years ahead, not just to the decree

Outside the office

Claire volunteers with a (fictional) Saturday legal information clinic, coaches youth soccer, and can usually be found on weekends walking the trails along the Arkansas River.
