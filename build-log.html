Demonstration project. Not a law firm. Sampson Family Law is a fictional firm created by a law student for a law school course on AI and the Law. It is not a real law firm and has no office, staff, or clients. Every person named on this site, including Ben Sampson, Libby Sampson, Jane, Tater, and every client, is a fictional character. The people in the photographs are the site owner's family and friends (and dog) playing those roles; their photos are used with permission of the owner and do not depict real attorneys, staff, or clients. No one on this site is a licensed attorney, and the creator is not licensed to practice law in Oklahoma or any other jurisdiction.

No legal advice. No attorney-client relationship. Nothing on this site is legal advice, and using it does not create an attorney-client relationship. Statutes and procedures change; information here is general and may be incomplete or out of date. For help with a real legal matter in Oklahoma, contact Legal Aid Services of Oklahoma or a licensed attorney.

Fictional details. The address, suite, phone number (555 numbers are reserved for fiction), hours, testimonials, client stories, characters, case outcomes, team members, credentials, and badges are invented for the demonstration. They are not real reviews, awards, bar memberships, or certifications. The consultation form on the contact page is a live class demonstration: submissions are emailed to the student presenter, so do not include sensitive or confidential information. The payments page takes no payments: its handles are fictional, and its QR codes open a demonstration page on this site, not any payment app or account. Statistics on the evidence page are real and cited to their public sources. Character photographs are personal photos used with permission of the owner; other photographs are free-licensed.
