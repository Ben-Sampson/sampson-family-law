Welcome

A family law practice built on listening first

When a marriage ends or a parenting arrangement stops working, people rarely need a lecture on the law. They need someone to slow things down, explain what is likely to happen next, and help them make good decisions while emotions run high.

That is the practice Sampson Family Law imagines: a small Tulsa office where clients reach their attorney directly, receive a written plan after the first meeting, and are never surprised by a bill. We favor agreement where it is safe and realistic, and prepare every case as though it might be heard by a judge.

  • Direct access to your attorney, not a call center
  • A written roadmap and budget after your first meeting
  • Mediation-first approach, courtroom-ready preparation

How we work

A clear path from first call to final order

Consultation

We listen to your story, identify urgent issues, and outline realistic options.

Roadmap

You receive a written plan with likely timelines, decisions ahead, and a fee estimate.

Resolution

We negotiate or mediate where it makes sense and prepare thoroughly for court where it does not.

Moving forward

We explain your final orders and help you plan for modifications or enforcement later.
