Payment FAQ

Questions about paying

What is a trust account, and why does my retainer go there?

Lawyers must keep money that belongs to clients, such as an unearned retainer, separate from the firm's own money, usually in a client trust (IOLTA) account. Funds move to the firm only as work is performed and billed.

Will I get a receipt?

Yes. We email an itemized receipt the same business day showing the invoice, amount, payment method, and remaining balance. Every payment also appears on your monthly statement.

Can I pay part of an invoice?

Yes. Partial payments are always welcome, and your balance updates on your next statement. If a payment plan would help, just ask; we're happy to set one up.

Is it safe to pay with a payment app?

Yes, as long as you use the handle printed on your invoice. Payment-app transfers can be hard to reverse, so if a handle ever looks different from what's on your invoice, call our office before sending anything. We will never ask you to pay by text message or gift card.

Who do I call with billing questions?

Call our office at (918) 555-0142 (Monday through Thursday 8:30 to 5:30, Friday 8:30 to 4:00), or reply to any statement email. We'll walk you through your invoice line by line.
