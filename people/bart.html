Bart K.

Tulsa

I was rear-ended on I-44 on my way to the course and couldn't work for months. Ben dealt with the insurance company and kept me updated the whole way. I'm back caddying now, and I didn't have to fight that battle alone.

The story

Bart was on his way to the golf course where he works as a caddie when a distracted driver rear-ended him in stop-and-go traffic on I-44 near Yale Avenue. The collision left him with injuries that required months of physical therapy and kept him off the course.

He came to Ben for help with the insurance claim. Ben handled the conversations with the carrier, tracked the medical bills, and kept Bart informed as the case moved forward.

The matter settled without going to trial. Bart is back caddying.

Fictional client and fictional story, played by a member of the site owner's family or circle of friends for a class demonstration. Not a real case or result. Past results do not guarantee future outcomes.
