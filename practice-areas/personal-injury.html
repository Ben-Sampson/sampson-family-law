Personal Injury

Steady help with insurers, medical bills, and lost income after an injury.

After a wreck or injury

The weeks after an injury are often the hardest time to deal with paperwork. Medical appointments, missed work, and calls from adjusters arrive all at once. Our role is to take that weight off: gather records, communicate with insurers, and make sure the full cost of the injury is understood before anything is signed.

Oklahoma generally applies a two-year statute of limitations to personal injury claims, and Oklahoma follows a modified comparative negligence rule. Both can significantly affect a claim, which is why early advice matters.

What a claim can include

A claim may cover medical expenses, lost wages, reduced earning capacity, and pain and suffering. Documentation is what turns those categories into numbers an insurer must take seriously.

Recovery comes first. The paperwork can wait for us.

FAQ

Personal Injury questions, answered

How long do I have to file?

Generally two years in Oklahoma for most personal injury claims, with exceptions. Ask early.

Should I talk to the other driver's insurer?

It is usually wise to speak with counsel before giving a recorded statement.
